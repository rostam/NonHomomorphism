A zoo of graphs in the profile space

Each graph G is placed by its profile φT(G) = α(T,G)/e(T) over the 85 test cores T. Most graphs here are not cores: trees, grids, hypercubes, multipartite graphs, unions, products, random graphs and more. The profile depends only on the homomorphism class, so every graph lands exactly on the point of its core whenever that core is a test graph. Many graphs therefore share a point; "spread" separates them slightly. Hover for details; the table below lists where every graph lands.
