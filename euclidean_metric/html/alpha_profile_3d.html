The α-profile space of graphs

Each graph G is the point φ(G) = (wT·α(T,G)/e(T))T over 85 test cores T, with χ-balanced weights wT. The distance is plain Euclidean distance, and the axes are the first three principal components. Complete graphs are the large circles joined by a line. Generalized Petersen graphs GP(n,k), 3 ≤ n ≤ 12, are the black diamonds. Circular cliques Kp/q (p ≤ 13) are the purple-edged squares, joined by a dashed line in order of p/q. Graphs that land on the same point are listed together. Drag to rotate; hover for names.
